[Gauged: A Wire Addon](https://modrinth.com/mod/gauged-a-wire-addon)
=======

> How big of a wire can I get?

This mod adds various wire gauges and adds them to Create: Power Grid for Minecraft 1.21.1 and Minecraft 1.20.1, adding wires with the smallest gauge being 14 AWG which is approximately 2mm² to the biggest of 2000 MCM which is over ***1000 mm²***.
These wires don't joke around!

## What Gets added in this mod:

- Copper wires with gauges from 14 AWG to 2000 MCM (2mm² to 1014mm²)
- A creative wire and cord variant with very little resistance and a ludicrous ampacity
- Aluminum wires with gauges from 14 AWG to 2000 MCM (2mm² to 1014mm²)
- Copper and Aluminum Cords with gauges from 14 AWG up to 1 AWG
